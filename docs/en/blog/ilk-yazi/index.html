Hi, I'm Enes

November 16, 2026, 1 min read

  • personal

For a long time I wanted a single place to talk about myself and what I do. This site is that place. In this first post, let me tell you a little about myself.

Where am I from?

I’m from Yozgat, and I lived and grew up in Istanbul.

Why social work?

What influenced my decision to study social work the most is that I am disabled myself. As a disabled person, being able to reach people and do something for them means a lot to me.

My time in England

I took a language course in England for a little over a month, and I would happily give the experience a 10 out of 10. The city I stayed in was designed to be accessible for disabled people; I could do everything with ease. That is the part that stayed with me the most.

On social media

I also have an account where I share content about disability: @enesdnmz_8. If you’re curious, come and say hi there too.

My goal

My biggest goal for the coming years is to travel abroad more and see new places. I’ll share the places I visit here as well.
