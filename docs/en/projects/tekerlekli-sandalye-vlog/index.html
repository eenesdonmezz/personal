Wheelchair Vlog

TikTok: @enesdnmz8

On Instagram and TikTok I share my daily life, my travels and the accessibility situations I come across as a wheelchair user. My aim is to make visible what makes life easier or harder for disabled people, and to raise awareness.

Accessibility is not a privilege; it is everyone’s right.
