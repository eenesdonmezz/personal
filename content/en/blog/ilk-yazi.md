For a long time I wanted a single place to talk about myself and what I do.
This site is that place. In this first post, let me tell you a little about
myself.

## Where am I from?

I'm from Yozgat, and I lived and grew up in Istanbul.

## Why social work?

What influenced my decision to study social work the most is that I am
disabled myself. As a disabled person, being able to reach people and do
something for them means a lot to me.

## My time in England

I took a language course in England for a little over a month, and I would
happily give the experience a 10 out of 10. The city I stayed in was designed
to be accessible for disabled people; I could do everything with ease. That
is the part that stayed with me the most.
