On Instagram and TikTok I share my daily life, my travels and the
accessibility situations I come across as a wheelchair user. My aim is to
make visible what makes life easier or harder for disabled people, and to
raise awareness.

## In numbers

- More than **1.2 million views** in total across TikTok and Instagram
- Most viewed video: **633,000** views (TikTok and Instagram)
- More than 600 followers on Instagram
